FAQ

Are nicotine pouches tobacco-free?

Yes — ZYN, Rogue, Velo and On! are tobacco-leaf-free. They still contain nicotine, which is addictive.

How many pouches are in a can?

All four brands come in cans of 20 pouches.

What is the strongest of these?

On! offers an 8 mg option; Velo goes up to 7 mg; ZYN and Rogue top out at 6 mg per pouch.
